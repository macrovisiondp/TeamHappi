# M9.2a: Vacancy share links and QR codes

Phase A of vacancy sharing, approved by the product owner on 29 October 2026 with the five recommendations:
1. one unlisted link per vacancy;
2. a "how to apply" text instead of an online form;
3. only chosen fields published, with pay only when ticked;
4. counts of opens only;
5. managed by anyone who can manage the vacancy.

**No applicant data is collected.** Online applications on the same link are M9.3 (gate R5), so printed QR codes keep working when that arrives.

## What it does

- **Share panel** on each vacancy (for people who can manage it):
  - **Create share link:** gives `<app>/jobs/<code>`, where the code is 12 random characters (unguessable, short enough for a poster).
  - **Copy:** copy the link.
  - **Download the QR code:** as PNG (1024 px) or SVG. The QR code encodes the link with `?s=qr`, so scans are counted separately.
  - **Print poster:** an A4 "We're hiring" page with the title, site, type, pay (if shown), a large QR code and the link. It prints without the app's navigation.
  - **Public details:** the "How to apply" text (up to 1000 characters) and "Show pay on the link" (off by default).
  - **Opens:** counts from the link and from the QR code. Counts only, nothing about who opened it.
  - **New link** (with a warning): replaces the code, so the old link and printed QR codes stop working. **Remove link** switches it off entirely.
- **Public page** (`/jobs/<code>`):
  - **No sign-in.** It isn't indexed by search engines, and it sets no cookies or tracking.
  - **When the vacancy is open, it shows:** the organisation's name, title, site, employment type, description, closing date, pay only if ticked, and how to apply, plus "It doesn't collect any of your information."
  - **When the vacancy is paused, filled, closed or past its closing date:** "This role is no longer accepting applications", showing only the title and organisation.
  - **Unknown, removed or replaced codes, and suspended organisations:** "Page not found".

## Security

- **How the public page reads data:** it has no signed-in organisation, so it reads through one database function, `app.public_vacancy(code, source)`.
  - **Built like the invitation preview:** SECURITY DEFINER, with a pinned `search_path`, and executable by the app role only.
  - **What it returns:** only the published fields of an open vacancy. Hiring manager, ids, team, internal notes and applicants are never returned.
  - **Malformed codes** are rejected before any lookup.
- **Counters:** change only through that function. The app role has no UPDATE grant on them.
- **What the app can change:** it can update only `public_code`, `how_to_apply` and `show_pay` (column grants).
- **Audit:** creating, replacing and removing links, and changing public details, are audited **without the code**, so reading the audit trail never reveals a working link.
- **Downloads:** QR downloads (`/recruitment/vacancies/<id>/qr`) and the poster need a signed-in member who can see, or manage, the vacancy. Everyone else gets "not found". Responses are `no-store`.
- **Rate limiting (a change from what I proposed):** the page is read-only and the codes are unguessable (62¹² possibilities), so in Phase A the main abuse is inflating the view counts. That's cosmetic, so **rate limiting is deferred to M9.3**, when the page starts accepting submissions (with Turnstile and Postgres-backed limits, as planned).

## Database: `20261102120000_vacancy_sharing`

- **New columns on `vacancy`:**
  - `public_code` (12 base62 characters; unique across organisations when set);
  - `how_to_apply` (1–1000 characters);
  - `show_pay` (default false);
  - `share_views_link` and `share_views_qr` (default 0).
- **New grant:** column UPDATE on `public_code`, `how_to_apply` and `show_pay` for the app role.
- **New function:** `app.public_vacancy(text, text)` (SECURITY DEFINER), executable by the app role only.
- **Existing data:** unchanged. The new columns get their defaults, and no vacancy is shared until someone creates a link.

## Tests

- **`tests/security/vacancy-sharing.test.ts`** (real app role):
  - **Permissions:** who can create, regenerate and remove links; workers and other organisations are refused; the code never appears in the audit trail.
  - **What the public lookup returns:** only published fields while open; drafts and closed vacancies show "closed"; pay only when ticked; the length limit on "how to apply".
  - **Counts:** link and QR opens are counted and previews aren't; the app role can't change counts.
  - **Codes:** malformed and unknown codes are refused; a replaced or removed code stops working; suspended organisations are hidden; only the app role can call the function; 2000 generated codes are unique and well formed.
- **`tests/security/db-roles.test.ts`:** the vacancy column grants.
- **`tests/e2e/vacancy-sharing.spec.ts`** (desktop and mobile, axe on every screen):
  1. The owner creates and opens a vacancy, creates a link, sets "how to apply", and downloads the QR code (a real PNG).
  2. The poster shows the vacancy.
  3. A signed-out visitor sees the details without pay and with the "doesn't collect" notice.
  4. **New link** makes the old one show "Page not found".

## Not in M9.2a

- **Online applications and a careers page** listing all vacancies (M9.3).
- **Short custom slugs** (for example `/jobs/harbour-barista`).
- **Social-media preview images.**
- **Rate limiting** (with M9.3).
