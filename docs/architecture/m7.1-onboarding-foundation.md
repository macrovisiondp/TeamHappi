# M7.1: Onboarding foundation

Implements the first increment of the approved M7 scope (`m7-onboarding-scope.md`), with the product owner's decisions D1–D5 and additional requirements 1–8.

## What it does

- **Templates** (`/onboarding/templates`; owners, admins, HR):
  - **Scope:** a template applies to the whole organisation, one site or one job title.
  - **Tasks:** each task has a type, who does it, a due offset from the start date, and whether it's required.
  - **Versions:** every change makes a new version.
  - **Suggested templates:** editable UK hospitality templates (kitchen, front of house, bar), added on request and clearly labelled "suggested". They are not legal advice and contain no right-to-work step.
- **Onboarding** (`/onboarding`, `/onboarding/new`, `/onboarding/[id]`, and a card on each person's page):
  - **Starting:** onboarding starts from an **employee record**, so no login is needed. The template's tasks are **copied** (a snapshot), with due dates worked out from the start date. Later template edits never change onboarding already under way.
  - **Tasks:** they can be assigned and given new due dates, marked done, reopened, or given a recorded **exception** with a reason. Tasks for the employee are recorded as done **on their behalf** until the employee view exists (M7.3).
- **Three separate states** (requirement 2), shown separately and never merged:

  | State | Meaning |
  |---|---|
  | Task progress | Tasks finished (done or exception) out of the total |
  | Operational readiness | Every **required** task is done or has an exception |
  | Final sign-off | A recorded decision by an owner, administrator or HR manager (D5) |

- **Right to work** is not part of M7.1. Every onboarding view says so: progress and sign-off "don't show whether someone has the right to work". Nothing claims legal compliance (requirements 1, 3 and 4).
- **Dashboard:** counts of people in progress, starting in the next 7 days, overdue tasks and people ready for sign-off; filters by status and site; progress bars.

## Permissions (new)

| Code | Owner | Admin | HR manager | Site manager | Recruiter | Worker |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `onboarding.view` | ✔ | ✔ | ✔ | ✔ (their sites) | – | – |
| `onboarding.manage` | ✔ | ✔ | ✔ | ✔ (their sites) | – | – |
| `onboarding.template.manage` | ✔ | ✔ | ✔ | – | – | – |
| `onboarding.signoff` | ✔ | ✔ | ✔ | – | – | – |

`onboarding.signoff` covers final sign-off **and** exceptions to required tasks (the "audited policy-based exceptions" allowed by D2). Administrators hold it because their built-in role has every permission. Nobody can sign off their own onboarding.

## Rules enforced on the server (`src/modules/onboarding`)

- **Site scope:** each onboarding takes the employee's primary site. Site-scoped permissions apply only there, and people without a site need organisation-wide permission. Anything outside your scope, or in another organisation, returns "not found".
- **Starting:** one open onboarding per person, never for people who have left. An archived template, or another organisation's template, employee, site or member, is rejected.
- **Exceptions:** always need a reason. Required tasks need `onboarding.signoff`.
- **Final sign-off:** only when operationally ready, by `onboarding.signoff`, not for your own record. Signed-off and cancelled onboarding can't be changed.
- **Audit:** every change writes to the append-only tenant audit trail, with ids and codes only. Free-text reasons and notes stay on the record, never in the audit detail.

## Database: `20261025120000_onboarding_foundation`

- **New tables:** four tenant-owned tables (`onboarding_template`, `onboarding_template_task`, `onboarding_case`, `onboarding_task`). Each has:
  - RLS ENABLE + FORCE with one tenant policy;
  - `UNIQUE(tenant_id, id)` and composite foreign keys;
  - SELECT and INSERT only, plus **column-level UPDATE**. A case's employee, template snapshot and start date, and a task's copied content, can't be changed.
  - **No DELETE.**
- **CHECK constraints:**
  - sign-off and cancellation must record who and when;
  - done and exception tasks must record who and when;
  - exceptions need a reason;
  - template scope must match its site or job title.
- **Indexes:** a partial unique index enforces one open case per employee.
- **Permissions:** four rows in `permission`, and `role_permission` rows for every organisation's **built-in** roles. This is the only change to existing data. Custom roles aren't touched.

## Tests

- `tests/unit/onboarding-policy.test.ts`:
  - progress, readiness, overdue, sign-off and exception rules;
  - calendar due dates;
  - organisation time zone;
  - template matching;
  - the suggested templates contain no right-to-work wording;
  - the built-in role permissions.
- `tests/security/onboarding.test.ts` (real app role):
  - who can manage templates; suggested templates are added once;
  - snapshots: editing a template doesn't change onboarding already started;
  - one open onboarding per person;
  - site-manager scope, workers locked out, and full cross-organisation isolation, including foreign ids;
  - completion "on behalf"; who can make exceptions;
  - readiness versus sign-off; no self sign-off; closed onboarding can't change;
  - cancellation needs a reason; audit events written with no free text;
  - no DELETE, protected columns, status CHECKs that hold even for the database owner, and permission rows matching the code.
- `tests/security/db-roles.test.ts`: posture (table and column grants, RLS coverage).
- `tests/e2e/onboarding.spec.ts`:
  - **covers:** an owner adds the suggested templates, starts onboarding for a person with no login from their record, records an exception, completes every task (one on the employee's behalf), sees readiness, then gives final sign-off; a worker can't see onboarding;
  - **runs on:** desktop and mobile, with accessibility checks on every screen.
