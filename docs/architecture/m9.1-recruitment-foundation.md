# M9.1: Recruitment foundation

The first increment of the approved M9 scope (`m9-recruitment-scope.md`), with the product owner's approval of decisions D1–D6 (29 October 2026). Local development only: nothing has been run on Neon, and no AWS resource, public page or email exists.

## What it does

- **Vacancies** (`/recruitment`, `/recruitment/vacancies/new`, `/recruitment/vacancies/[id]`):
  - **Fields:** title, job title (copied to the person on hire), site, optional team, employment type, openings, closing date, hiring manager, description, and optional pay (a decimal range per hour or year, D5).
  - **Status:** draft → open, paused, filled or closed, with every change audited. When the number of hires reaches the number of openings, the page suggests marking the vacancy filled.
  - **List:** filters by status and site, with counts per stage.
- **Applicants:** added by managers on the vacancy page.
  - **Duplicate check:** "Check for an existing candidate" by email shows any people already using that address.
  - **No automatic merging (D1):** a new candidate with the same email is refused until the manager either picks the existing candidate or ticks "This is a different person". The same candidate can't apply twice to the same vacancy.
  - **Minimal data:** name, email, phone and source only. The form reminds managers not to record date of birth, nationality, photos or right-to-work details.
- **Pipeline (D2):** Applied → Screening → Shortlisted → Interview → Offer.
  - **Moving:** between working stages (backwards too) with a stage selector.
  - **Closing:** **Reject** (needs a reason) and **Withdrawn by candidate** close an application from any working stage. **Hire** only from Offer.
  - **Renaming:** each organisation can rename the working stages in Recruitment → Settings. Hired, Rejected and Withdrawn keep their names.
  - **History:** every stage change is recorded in an append-only history.
- **Notes:** append-only, with a reminder that candidates can ask to see them.
- **Interviews:**
  - **Scheduling:** date and time in the organisation's time zone (clock changes handled), length, format, where, and one or more interviewers.
  - **Changes:** cancel, no-show, and outcome (progress, hold or don't progress) with feedback.
  - **Schedule page:** `/recruitment/interviews`, the internal schedule for the last week and the next eight weeks. No external calendar.
  - **Interviewers without recruitment access** (for example, a chef sitting in) see only their own interviews: the candidate's name, the vacancy and the time. They can record their outcome, but not contact details, notes, history or the pipeline.
- **Hire** (D3, D4): needs `recruitment.hire` **and** `employee.manage` at the vacancy's site (owners, admins and HR, not site managers or recruiters).
  - **What it does, in one transaction:**
    - **Creates the employee record:** status pending, a new employee number, the name, email and phone from the application, the job title, site and team from the vacancy, and the start date entered at hire.
    - **Marks the application hired**, linking it to that record.
    - **Optionally starts onboarding** with the chosen template, pre-selected when the hirer can manage onboarding.
  - **All or nothing:** if onboarding can't start, nothing is created.
  - **No duplicates:** a retry or double click returns the same employee. If two hires race, one claims the application and the other is rolled back.
  - **No login:** an invitation does that, from the person page (M7.3).
  - **Link kept:** the person page shows "Hired from".
- **Data review** (`/recruitment/review`; owners, admins and HR, D6):
  - **Who appears:** candidates whose last application ended more than the retention period ago (6 months by default, configurable from 3 to 12).
  - **What anonymising does:** removes the name and contact details, redacts notes and clears interview feedback and location, keeping stage history for reporting. It's audited and can't be undone.
  - **Nothing happens automatically.**
- **Email:** none. Every relevant screen says so ("No emails are sent to candidates yet… tell the candidate yourself").

## Permissions

| Code | Owner / admin | HR manager | Recruiter | Site manager | Worker |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `recruitment.view` | ✔ | ✔ | ✔ | ✔ (their sites) | – |
| `recruitment.manage` | ✔ | ✔ | ✔ | ✔ (their sites) | – |
| `recruitment.hire` (also needs `employee.manage`) | ✔ | ✔ | – | – | – |
| `recruitment.settings` | ✔ | ✔ | – | – | – |

- **Site scope:** follows the vacancy's site. Other sites, and other organisations, return "not found".
- **Interviewer access:** comes from being the named interviewer, not from a role.
- **Platform staff:** can't read any of these tables.

## Audit

- **Detail holds ids and codes only.** Never names, email addresses, phone numbers, notes, feedback or pay.
- **Events:**
  - **Vacancies and candidates:** `recruitment.vacancy_created`, `_updated`, `_status_changed`; `recruitment.candidate_created`; `recruitment.candidate_anonymised`.
  - **Applications:** `recruitment.application_added`; `recruitment.stage_changed`; `recruitment.application_rejected`, `_withdrawn`; `recruitment.note_added`; `recruitment.hired`.
  - **Interviews and settings:** `recruitment.interview_scheduled`, `_rescheduled`, `_cancelled`, `_no_show`, `_feedback`; `recruitment.settings_updated`.

## Database: `20261101120000_recruitment_foundation`

- **Eight tenant-owned tables:** `recruitment_settings`, `vacancy`, `candidate`, `application`, `application_event`, `application_note`, `interview` and `interview_interviewer`.
- **On every table:**
  - RLS ENABLE + FORCE with one tenant policy, and `UNIQUE(tenant_id, id)`;
  - composite foreign keys to site, team, membership, employee, candidate and vacancy, so nothing can point at another organisation's records;
  - column-level UPDATE and **no DELETE**.
- **Integrity rules:**
  - **One application** per candidate per vacancy, and one per hired employee.
  - **State check:** stage and outcome always agree, and hired, rejected and withdrawn applications record what they need.
  - **Pay check:** a non-negative range with a period, or nothing.
  - **Anonymised candidates** hold no contact details.
  - **Interview outcomes** need who and when.
  - **Retention period:** 3 to 12 months.
- **Triggers:**
  - **Stage history** is append-only for every role.
  - **A note** can only be redacted (its body cleared), once, and never edited or deleted.
- **Permissions:** four catalogue rows, and role-permission rows for every organisation's **built-in** roles. This is the only change to existing data.

## Tests

- **`tests/unit/recruitment-policy.test.ts`:** stage order, labels, moves, hire rules, retention dates (including month ends), pay parsing, time-zone conversion (GMT and BST, invalid input), and built-in role permissions.
- **`tests/security/recruitment.test.ts`** (real app role):
  - **Vacancies:** site scope for creating and listing; other organisations' sites refused; pay validation, with pay never in the audit trail.
  - **Applicants:** duplicate-email confirmation, no merging, and explicit reuse; the same vacancy twice refused; another organisation refused; no names or emails in the audit trail.
  - **Pipeline and notes:** stage rules; rejection reasons; closed applications; history; notes kept off the audit trail.
  - **Interviews:** scheduled in the organisation's time zone; the interviewer's limited view and feedback; non-interviewers refused; the schedule scope.
  - **Hire:** needs Offer and the right role (site managers and recruiters refused); rolls back when onboarding can't start; **two hires racing still create one employee**; a retry returns the same person; no login; onboarding started; team assigned; "Hired from"; audit without personal data.
  - **Retention:** not listed before the due date; anonymisation, by authorised roles only; history kept.
  - **Settings:** rules and limits.
  - **Database:** no DELETE; protected columns; the append-only and redact-only triggers; state and anonymisation checks for the database owner; a cross-organisation foreign key; permission rows.
- **`tests/security/db-roles.test.ts`:** posture for the eight tables.
- **`tests/e2e/recruitment.spec.ts`** (desktop and mobile, axe on every screen):
  - **Owner:** creates and opens a vacancy with pay; checks for an existing candidate; adds an applicant; moves to Interview; schedules and records an outcome; moves to Offer; hires with onboarding and lands on the new person record ("Hired from", "Open onboarding"); sees the schedule.
  - **Worker:** sent to their own interviews, and refused vacancy creation.

## Known limits and follow-ups

- **Candidate details can't be edited** after they're added (only anonymised). Add an "Edit contact details" action if needed.
- **The interviewer picker** lists every active member; a search would help large organisations.
