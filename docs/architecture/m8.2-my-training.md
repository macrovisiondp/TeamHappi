# M8.2: My training

The second increment of the approved M8 scope (`m8-training-scope.md`): the employee's own training on their phone. Approved for development by the product owner on 29 October 2026 ("proceed to next" after M8.1). **No database change:** it uses the M8.1 tables and grants.

## What it does

- **My training** (`/training/me`, phone first):
  - **Who can see it:** it's reached by **ownership** (the staff record linked to the signed-in member, M7.3), never by a role. Opening **Training** without manager access, but with a linked record, takes you there. Everyone else still sees "Your role doesn't include training".
  - **What it shows:**
    - "To do": due, overdue, expired and expiring courses, plus courses whose content changed since they confirmed it;
    - "Up to date": with "valid until" dates;
    - "My history": their own valid records ("Confirmed by you", or how a manager recorded it).
  - **What it never shows:** other people's training, voided mistakes (those stay with managers), or management screens.
- **Read and confirm** (D3), only for in-house courses set up as read-and-confirm that **apply to them** under the M8.1 rules:
  - **How it works:** they open "Read it", tick "I've read and understood …" and confirm.
  - **What's recorded:**
    - the course, the **content version** read, the date (in the organisation's time zone) and the method `read_and_confirm`;
    - self-completed = yes, and who recorded it (themselves);
    - an expiry worked out from the course's renewal period.
  - **Audit:** `training.self_confirmed`, with ids and the version only.
  - **Content changed while reading:** the confirmation is refused ("read the new version").
  - **Content changed since they confirmed:** it's offered again with "updated since you last confirmed". The earlier confirmation stays valid until it expires.
  - **What it isn't:** the screen says it isn't a test or a qualification.
- **External and in-person courses** (D5): staff can't record, confirm or verify them. The card says to show the certificate to their manager, or that the manager records it.
- **My onboarding:** a "Training to complete" list with a link to My training. Information only: onboarding rules are unchanged.
- **Partners:** none listed yet (M8.3), so no external opportunities or referral links are shown.

## Security

| Rule | Where |
|---|---|
| Access by the linked record only, and only while they're still employed | `self.ts` `ownRecord` |
| Confirm only read-and-confirm courses that apply to them, at the current version | `confirmMyCourse` |
| External or in-person: refused with an explanation; courses that don't apply, archived ones, and other organisations': "not found" | `confirmMyCourse` |
| Workers still can't view the matrix or anyone's training page, record, correct or void | M8.1 permissions (unchanged) |
| Records stay append-only (column grants and trigger from M8.1) | database |

## Tests

- **`tests/security/my-training.test.ts`** (real app role):
  - only linked records get access; other organisations see nothing;
  - only applicable courses appear; external courses have no content and can't be confirmed;
  - colleagues' records are invisible;
  - the tick and the current version are required;
  - the record has the right method, version, self flag, dates and recorder, and is audited;
  - updated content shows as changed;
  - external, inapplicable and other-organisation courses are refused;
  - workers can't record or void;
  - archived courses and people who have left drop out.
- **`tests/e2e/my-training.spec.ts`** (desktop and mobile, axe):
  1. The owner adds a read-and-confirm course with a rule for one new person, then invites them.
  2. They sign up; **Training** takes them to **My training**; they read, tick and confirm, and it moves to "Up to date", "Confirmed by you".
  3. The manager sees it as up to date, recorded as "Read and confirm".
- **Regression:** the employee onboarding browser tests pass with the new My onboarding section.
