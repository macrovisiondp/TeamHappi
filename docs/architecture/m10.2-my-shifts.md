# M10.2: My shifts (staff)

Built to the approved Rota scope (`m10-rota-scope.md`, decisions D2, D7 and D8). The product owner asked to start it on 2 November 2026.

## What it does

- **My shifts** (`/rota/me`), phone first, for anyone whose login is linked to their staff record:
  - their **published** shifts for this week and the next three, at any site;
  - for each shift: day, times, site, role, team, hours and break, and the manager's note;
  - **drafts are never shown** (D2);
  - **New** and **Changed** flags since they last looked, and a "No longer yours" list for shifts they were taken off or that were cancelled;
  - before their first visit, every upcoming shift counts as new, so the badge invites them in. Opening the page marks everything as seen, so the highlights clear next time.
- **Header badge** (D8): "2 shift updates" appears in the top bar when there's something new, and links to My shifts. Nothing is shown otherwise.
- **Open shifts** (D7):
  - staff see open, published, future shifts at **their own site**, and can **ask for one**, or withdraw;
  - managers see "Asked to take it" on each shift, and **Approve** or **Decline**;
  - **approving runs every assignment check:** right-to-work and double-booking blocks, and warnings that need a written reason;
  - **filling a shift, by approval or by direct assignment, answers every other request for it** (declined).
- **The Rota page for staff:** people with no rota access who have a linked record are sent from `/rota` to `/rota/me`.

## Privacy

- **Ownership, not roles:** access is through the employee record linked to the signed-in member; there's no new permission.
- **What staff never see:** who else is working, other people's requests, or the reason a manager gave for an override.
- **Audit (ids only):**
  - `rota.shift_requested`;
  - `rota.shift_request_withdrawn`;
  - `rota.shift_request_declined`;
  - approvals appear as `rota.shift_assigned`.

## Database: `20261106120000_my_shifts`

- **`shift_request`:**
  - links a shift and a person; status is pending, approved, declined or withdrawn, with who decided and when;
  - only one pending request per person per shift;
  - the shift and person can't change.
- **`rota_seen`:** one row per person, holding when they last looked.
- **Both tables:**
  - `tenant_id`;
  - RLS enabled and forced, with a tenant policy;
  - `UNIQUE(tenant_id, id)` and composite keys;
  - SELECT and INSERT, column-level UPDATE, and **no DELETE**.
- **No changes to existing tables or permissions.**

## Tests

- **`tests/security/my-shifts.test.ts`** (8 tests, as the app role):
  - an unlinked login sees nothing;
  - drafts are hidden;
  - new, then seen, then changed, then removed;
  - people see only their own shifts;
  - open shifts only at their own site, with no names;
  - one request at a time;
  - other sites and unlinked logins are refused;
  - managers see requests; approval runs the checks (right-to-work block); filling a shift answers the rest;
  - withdrawing only your own request; declines are audited;
  - no DELETE, and fixed columns.
- **`tests/e2e/my-shifts.spec.ts`** (desktop and mobile, with accessibility checks):
  1. a new person with a right-to-work check is given a shift in next week's published rota;
  2. they accept an invite and see the header badge;
  3. `/rota` takes them to My shifts, with the shift marked New;
  4. they ask for an open shift, and the manager approves;
  5. it then shows as theirs, marked New.
- **Updated:** `db-roles` (reviewed privileges and RLS list).
- **Results:**
  - full check: 560 tests and the build pass;
  - Rota, My shifts and app-screens browser tests pass. One mobile run hit the sign-in rate limit and passed on retry.
