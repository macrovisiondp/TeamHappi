# M8.1: Training foundation

The first increment of the approved M8 scope (`m8-training-scope.md`), following the product owner's combined approval and instructions (decisions D1–D6 approved). Local development only: nothing has been run on Neon.

## What it does

- **Course catalogue** (`/training/courses`; owners, admins and HR):
  - **Fields:** name, description, category, type (internal or external), completion method, whether a certificate is expected, renewal period, expiry warning (60 days by default) and archived status.
  - **Completion method:**
    - internal courses are **given in person** (a manager records them) or **read and confirm** (with versioned content, for staff self-service in M8.2);
    - external courses end with a **certificate**.
  - **Fixed once created:** the type and method, because records depend on them.
  - **Content versions:** in-house content is versioned; changing it makes a new version.
- **Suggested UK hospitality courses**, added on request, editable and labelled "(suggested)": food safety and hygiene (Level 2), allergen awareness, fire safety, health and safety induction, manual handling, first aid awareness, personal licence holder qualification, age verification (Challenge 25) and safeguarding awareness.
  - **No legal claims:** none is described as legally required, and renewal periods are common practice to confirm with an adviser or provider.
  - **No obligations:** they create **no rules**, so nobody is required to do anything until the business adds one.
- **Requirement rules** on each course page:
  - **Applies to:** everyone, a site, a job title, or a named person (D1, never an access role);
  - **Strength:** mandatory or recommended;
  - **Due:** within N days.
- **Training matrix** (`/training`): people by required courses, with the status in each cell.
  - **Counts:** people up to date, overdue, expired and expiring.
  - **Filters:** site, team, job title and status.
  - **On a phone:** the table scrolls sideways inside its own region, with the person column fixed.
- **Person training** (`/people/[id]/training`), linked from the matrix and the person page: their requirements and status, **Record training**, and the full history.
  - **Each record shows:** completion and expiry, how it was done, provider, certificate reference, and who recorded it and when.
  - **Corrections:** **Correct** (void and replace, starting from the original record's details) and **Void** (with a reason). Both stay in the history.
- **Person page:** a Training card (summary and anything needing attention).
- **Onboarding page:** a Training card **for information only**. It says outright that training doesn't change "cleared to start" or sign-off (D4). M7 rules are unchanged.

## How requirements are worked out (D2)

There is no assignment table. Requirements are calculated from the rules **every time they're read** (`src/modules/training/policy.ts`, unit-tested). Every trigger in D2 is therefore reflected immediately, with no background job or event system:

| Change | Effect |
|---|---|
| Joins (new employee record) | Organisation, site, job-title and named rules apply at once |
| Changes job title | Job-title rules are re-matched (ignoring capitals and spaces at the ends) |
| Moves site | Site rules for the new site apply; the old site's stop |
| A rule is added, edited or removed | Applies to everyone it matches on the next read |
| A course is archived | Its rules stop applying, and it leaves the matrix |
| Leaves (`status = left`) | No requirements; the history is kept |

**Overlapping rules.** Several rules for the same course make **one** requirement, so there are no duplicates:
- it's **mandatory** if any applicable rule is mandatory;
- it's due on the **earliest** due date among the applicable rules of that strength (only the mandatory rules, if any are).

**Due date.** `due_days` after the **later** of the person's start date and the date the rule took effect (`effective_from`, the day it was created).
- **New starters** are due N days after they start.
- **Existing staff** are due N days after the rule is added, so they aren't overdue the moment a rule appears.
- **People with no start date** count from the rule's date.
- **Known limitation:** the system doesn't record when someone moved site or changed job title. A person who moves into a rule's scope is counted from the rule's original date, so they may show as overdue straight away. That's deliberate, because they do need the training. A move history could refine this later.

**Status for one person and course** (D6):
- **Up to date:** a valid record (not voided, not expired) that isn't within the course's warning window.
- **Expiring soon:** a valid record expiring within the warning window. The expiry day itself still counts as valid.
- **Expired:** the latest record has expired. **An expired certificate never counts as valid because it was once completed.**
- **Due / Overdue:** no record yet, and the due date is today or later / has passed.
- **Not required:** a record exists but no rule applies. It's kept in the history and doesn't appear in the matrix.

A person is **up to date** when no **mandatory** requirement is overdue or expired. Dates use the organisation's time zone for "today" and plain calendar dates for everything else.

**Expiry.** Taken from the certificate if the manager enters one. Otherwise it's the completion date plus the course's renewal period in calendar months, clamped to the month's end (31 January + 1 month = 28 or 29 February). With no renewal period, it never expires.

## Recording and corrections (D3, D5)

- **Who records:** managers with `training.record` (owners, admins, HR; site managers at their sites only), for current staff.
  - external courses are recorded as an **external certificate**;
  - internal courses as **given in person**.
- **Read-and-confirm isn't recorded on anyone's behalf.** It's reserved for the person's own confirmation in M8.2, and is never presented as an exam, accreditation or qualification.
- **Employees can't record anything** in M8.1.
- **Records are append-only facts:**
  - the app can only UPDATE the void columns;
  - a database trigger blocks any change to a record's facts, any change after a void, and any DELETE, for every role.
- **Corrections:** a correction voids the old record with a reason and adds a replacement that points to it (`replaces_id`, same person only), in one transaction.
- **Data minimisation:** certificate references are short labels ("only a certificate number"). No uploads (G7).

## Permissions

| Code | Owner | Admin | HR manager | Site manager | Recruiter | Worker |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `training.view` | ✔ | ✔ | ✔ | ✔ (their sites) | – | – |
| `training.record` | ✔ | ✔ | ✔ | ✔ (their sites) | – | – |
| `training.manage` | ✔ | ✔ | ✔ | – | – | – |

- **Site scope:** follows the person's primary site. People without a site need the permission organisation-wide.
- **Cross-site and cross-organisation:** other sites' people, and other organisations' anything, return "not found".
- **Staff self-service** ("My training", by ownership) is M8.2.

## External training partners (prepared, not built)

`src/modules/training/providers.ts` fixes the shape for M8.3 without building it.
- **Partner listings** (platform-managed) and **partner courses** map to a course **category** or to an organisation's course. They're never an organisation's own course and never a completion. A referral click never marks anything complete.
- **Future provider API capabilities** (catalogue sync, enrolment, progress, completion, certificate verification) are declared as an **interface only**. Each needs separate approval, a privacy assessment and a lawful basis before any personal data is sent.
- **`referralUrl(base, referralId)`** builds a safe outbound link:
  - https only, a public host name, no embedded credentials;
  - refuses query keys that identify a person or organisation (email, name, employee, membership, tenant and so on);
  - adds only the approved referral identifier;
  - is unit-tested with unsafe and identifying URLs.

  No partner data or links are shown in M8.1.

## Database: `20261031120000_training_foundation`

- **Three tenant-owned tables:**
  - **`training_course`:** the catalogue.
  - **`training_requirement`:** the rules.
  - **`training_record`:** completions.
- **On every table:** RLS ENABLE + FORCE with one tenant policy, `UNIQUE(tenant_id, id)`, composite foreign keys (rules can't point at another organisation's course, site or person; records can't point at another organisation's person or course), column-level UPDATE and **no DELETE**.
- **CHECK constraints:**
  - category list;
  - type matches method;
  - ranges (renewal 1–120 months, warning 0–365 days, due 0–365 days);
  - a rule's scope matches its target;
  - expiry on or after completion;
  - a void needs who, when and a reason.
- **Indexes and triggers:**
  - one active rule per course and target (partial unique index);
  - one active course per name;
  - the void-once trigger.
- **Permissions:** three catalogue rows, and role-permission rows for every organisation's **built-in** roles. This is the only change to existing data.
