# M7.2: Onboarding compliance

Implements the second increment of the approved M7 scope (`m7-onboarding-scope.md`): recording right-to-work checks, a document checklist (metadata only), and a computed "cleared to start". It follows decisions D1 (site managers don't record or see right-to-work details) and D4 (no share codes or document numbers).

**TeamHappi records the employer's check. It never makes the legal decision.** All right-to-work wording and rules below need review by a qualified adviser before this goes live (gate **G4**).

## What it does

- **Right-to-work record** (`/people/[id]/right-to-work`; owners, admins and HR only):
  - **What's recorded:**
    - **The check itself:** how it was done (manual check of original documents, Home Office online check, or identity service provider), the Home Office list for manual checks (A, B group 1, B group 2), the date and who did it, and whether the person was present.
    - **The result:** the outcome (`confirmed`, `time_limited`, `not_confirmed`), plus the date permission ends and the follow-up date for time-limited permission.
    - **Free text:** an optional note.
  - **Never stored:** document numbers, share codes, nationality or copies. Notes and reasons that look like a document number or share code are refused.
  - **Append-only:** a follow-up check or a correction (with a reason) becomes the current record, and earlier ones stay in the history. Nothing can be edited or deleted, by the app or by anyone else (database trigger).
  - **Audited views:** every view of the record writes `rtw.record_viewed`. Links to it never prefetch, so hovering doesn't count as a view.
- **Follow-ups** (`/onboarding/right-to-work`, a new tab; audited): people whose follow-up is due within 60 days or overdue, whose permission has ended, or whose check couldn't be confirmed, plus people with no check recorded (so existing staff can be added). The dashboard shows only a **count**, which isn't audited.
- **Document checklist** (on each onboarding page; site managers can use it):
  - **What's recorded:** that a document such as a food hygiene certificate or personal licence was **seen** or **verified**, by whom, when, and its expiry date. A document can also be rejected or waived, with a reason. No file is stored, and the `document_id` column is reserved for when storage is approved (G7).
  - **Rules:** right-to-work documents ("passport", "visa", "share code" and so on) are refused here and belong in the right-to-work record. Waiving a **required** document needs `onboarding.signoff`, as for required tasks.
- **Cleared to start**, worked out on the server and never ticked by hand. A person is cleared when:
  - every required task is done or has an exception;
  - every required document is seen, verified (and not expired) or waived;
  - a recorded right-to-work check covers the date.

  The date is the start date, or today once they've started. **Site managers see yes/no and what's missing, never the record itself.** This doesn't merge M7.1's three states (task progress, operational readiness, final sign-off): it's shown separately, and final sign-off is unchanged.
- **For Rota later:** `isClearedToWork(employeeId, date)` returns yes/no only.

## What "covers the date" means

The rules apply to the current check (`compliance-policy.ts`, unit-tested):

| State | When | Covers? |
|---|---|---|
| No check recorded | Nothing recorded, or checked after the date | No |
| Recorded as not confirmed | Outcome `not_confirmed` | No |
| Permission has ended | Time-limited, end date before the date | No |
| Follow-up overdue | Follow-up date passed, permission not yet ended | Yes, flagged |
| Follow-up due soon | Follow-up within 60 days | Yes, flagged |
| Check recorded | Otherwise | Yes |

**Consistency rules for a new check**, which stop records that contradict themselves (also for G4 review):
- List A means confirmed; List B means time-limited.
- Identity service checks are continuous only.
- A confirmed check needs the person present.
- Check dates can't be in the future.
- Time-limited permission needs an end date after the check, and a follow-up on or before it (by default, the end date).

## Audit

- **Detail holds ids and the kind of record only.** The method, list, outcome, dates, notes and reasons never go into the audit trail.
- **Right to work:** `rtw.check_recorded`, `rtw.record_viewed` and `rtw.follow_ups_viewed`.
- **Documents:** `onboarding.document_added`, `_seen`, `_verified`, `_rejected`, `_waived` and `_reopened`.

## Database: `20261029120000_onboarding_compliance`

- **`rtw_check`** (tenant-owned):
  - **Isolation:** RLS ENABLE + FORCE, one tenant policy, `UNIQUE(tenant_id, id)`, composite foreign keys.
  - **Append-only:** SELECT and INSERT only for the app, and a trigger blocks UPDATE and DELETE for every role.
  - **One history per person:** one first check per person, each check superseded at most once, and a check can only supersede the same person's check (composite foreign key).
  - **CHECK constraints:** the list matches the method; time-limited dates are present and in order; corrections have reasons.
- **`onboarding_document`** (tenant-owned):
  - **Isolation:** the same RLS and keys.
  - **Grants:** column-level UPDATE of status, expiry and reason only (title, case and requirement never change), and no DELETE.
  - **CHECK constraints:** who and when are recorded for every status after "requested", and a reason for rejections and waivers.
- **Permissions:** two rows in `permission`, and `role_permission` rows for every organisation's built-in owner, admin and HR manager roles. This is the only change to existing data. Custom roles aren't touched.

## Not in M7.2

- **Linking template "document" tasks to the checklist.**
- **The employee's own view** (M7.3).
- **Automatic deletion:** none. Retention review is pending the adviser (IS-07).
