# M10.1: Rota foundation (managers)

Built to the approved scope in `m10-rota-scope.md`. The product owner approved every recommendation (D1 to D10) on 2 November 2026.

## What it does

- **Rota page** (`/rota`):
  - choose a site and move between weeks (Monday to Sunday, D1);
  - each day lists its shifts: times, role, team, hours and break, the person or **Open**, notes, and any checks.
- **Shifts:**
  - day, start and end, entered in the site's time zone; an end before the start means the next day;
  - unpaid break, optional role label (e.g. "Bar"), team at that site, and note;
  - edit, or cancel (kept for history);
  - **limits:** at most 16 hours; the break must be shorter than the shift; a time that doesn't exist because the clocks go forward is refused.
- **Assigning** (one person per shift, or open):
  - **Blocked:**
    - **no recorded right-to-work check covers the shift date** (D3). Onboarding's yes/no check is used, so site managers never see right-to-work details;
    - **the person is already on an overlapping shift** (at any site).
  - **Warnings, which need a written reason to go ahead** (D4, D5):
    - onboarding required tasks still open;
    - mandatory training overdue or expired **on the shift date**;
    - under 11 hours' rest;
    - over 6 hours with under 20 minutes' break;
    - over 48 hours in the rota week.

    The reason is kept on the assignment for managers. The audit records only the warning codes.
  - **Concurrency:** assignments for the same person are serialised (row lock), so two managers can't double-book them at once.
- **Copy last week** (into an empty week only):
  - same wall-clock times, so clock changes are handled; a time that doesn't exist that week is skipped and reported;
  - **people are copied only where every check passes;** the rest are left open and the count is reported.
- **Publish** (D2, D10):
  - needs `rota.publish` at the site;
  - drafts aren't visible to staff (staff views come in M10.2);
  - after publishing, every change to a shift (created, edited, cancelled, assigned, unassigned) goes into `shift_change`, append-only. That's what M10.2 will highlight and M11 will read.
- **Hours:**
  - per person for the week (shift length minus break), and the week's total and open shifts;
  - **hours only; no pay or cost** (D6).
- **Printable week** (`/print/rota`): person by day, plus open shifts, for people without a login (D8).
- **Plain notices on the page:**
  - working-time checks are guidance, not legal advice;
  - under-18 rules aren't checked (D9).

## Permissions

| Code | Owner, admin, HR | Site manager | Recruiter, worker |
|---|---|---|---|
| `rota.view`, `rota.manage`, `rota.publish` | all sites | their sites | none |

They're added to the catalogue, the organisation script and the migration. Other organisations, and sites outside your scope, get "not found".

## Cross-module calls

These are new, and read-only:
- **`onboarding.rtwCoverage(employeeIds)`:** yes/no per person and date.
- **`onboarding.openRequiredTasks(employeeIds, today)`:** count only, for onboardings the actor can view.
- **`training.mandatoryGapsOn(employeeIds, date)`:** course names only, for people whose training the actor can view.

## Database: `20261105120000_rota_foundation`

- **Four tables:**
  - **`rota_week`:** one per site and week; must start on a Monday; draft or published.
  - **`shift`:** belongs to a week **and the same site** (composite key); times, break, team, label, note; active or cancelled.
  - **`shift_assignment`:** one current per shift (partial unique index); removal is recorded, not deleted.
  - **`shift_change`:** append-only (trigger).
- **Each table has:**
  - `tenant_id`;
  - RLS enabled and forced, with a tenant policy;
  - `UNIQUE(tenant_id, id)` and composite keys;
  - SELECT and INSERT, column-level UPDATE, and **no DELETE**.
- **What can't change:** a shift's week and site, and an assignment's person.
- **Permissions:** three are added to built-in roles.

## Also in this change

**Test tooling:** besides the `beforeExit` hook (QA report, I-11), `embedded-postgres`'s `exit` hook is now removed too. That fixes a `done is not a function` error at the end of database test runs. Failing runs still exit 1.
