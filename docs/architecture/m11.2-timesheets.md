# M11.2 + M11.3: Timesheets, approval and export (design)

**Status:** built on 19 November 2026. The product owner bundled M11.2 and M11.3 into one release the same day. The migration is add-only, so it's applied to Neon dev under the product owner's standing approval for add-only dev migrations ([record](../operations/m11.2-neon-approval.md)).

**Scope:** [M11 scope](m11-attendance-scope.md), decisions D4, D6, D8, D9 and D11.

## 1. What people can do

**Managers** (Attendance → Timesheets, for their sites):
- See one site's week: planned hours (published rota) and worked hours (clocked time minus recorded breaks), per person and day, with totals.
- See each clocked time with its flags, including **No break recorded**: the shift planned a break but none was clocked. The planned break is shown and **never deducted** (D6).
- Correct times as before (M11.1) until the week is approved.
- **Approve** the week (`attendance.approve`, D8), once it has ended and nobody is still clocked in. Approving **locks** every time in that week at that site.
- **Reopen** an approved week with a reason (D9). Every approval and reopening is kept.

**Owners, admins and HR** (`attendance.export`, organisation-wide, D11): download a CSV of **approved weeks only**, for up to 93 days, across all sites. Each row is one clocked time:
- employee number, name, site, date;
- clock in and out, break minutes;
- worked minutes, worked hours as an exact decimal (e.g. `7.50`);
- whether it was on the rota.

There is no money in it. Cells starting with `=`, `+`, `-` or `@` are prefixed so spreadsheets don't run them. Every export is audited.

**Staff** see their approved hours per week on My shifts (last five weeks).

## 2. Data model (migration `20261109120000_attendance_timesheets`)

| Table | Holds | Rules in the database |
|---|---|---|
| `timesheet` | site, week start (Monday), status (`open`/`approved`), approved by and at | one per site and week; week start is a Monday; status and approver agree |
| `timesheet_event` | approved / reopened, reason (reopen only), who, when | append-only (trigger) |

**The lock is enforced twice:** in the service (a clear message), and by a trigger on `time_entry`, which refuses any insert or update whose old or new clock-in falls in an approved week at its site (worked out in the site's time zone).

**Permissions:**
- `attendance.approve`: owner, admin, HR manager, site manager.
- `attendance.export`: owner, admin, HR manager.

## 3. Security

- Site scope on every read and write. Other sites and organisations get "not found".
- Exporting needs organisation-wide `attendance.export`. Site managers can't export.
- Reasons stay in `timesheet_event` and `time_entry_change`, never in the audit trail.
- No pay, rates or money anywhere.

## 4. Not included

- Correcting break times (still clock-in and clock-out only).
- Offline clocking (later, with the installable app).
- Holidays and absence (Release 2).
