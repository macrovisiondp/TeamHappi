# M11.1: Clocking (design)

**Status:** built locally on 19 November 2026. The Neon migration needs the product owner's approval ([request](../operations/m11.1-neon-approval.md)).

**Scope:** [M11 scope](m11-attendance-scope.md), approved 19 November 2026 (D1–D11 as recommended). This increment covers clocking in and out, breaks, the "Who's in" view and manager corrections. Weekly timesheets and approval are M11.2; the CSV export is M11.3.

**Depends on:** M10.3 (built on its branch). It must be merged first.

## 1. What people can do

**Staff with a linked login** (My shifts page, D1):
- **Clock in.** The server takes the time; the phone's clock is never used. TeamHappi matches today's published shift they are on at any site, from 2 hours before it starts until it ends. With no match, the time is recorded at their home site and flagged "no shift" (D3).
- **Start and end a break** (D6), optional. Clocking out ends an open break at the same time.
- **Clock out.** If they have been clocked in for more than 24 hours, they're asked to see their manager instead (D7).
- See **their own times** for the last 14 days, including any manager corrections.

**Managers** on the Attendance page, for the sites they cover:
- **Who's in** for a site and a day: everyone rostered that day and everyone who clocked in, with a status and flags (`attendance.view`).
- **Record** times for anyone at the site, including staff without a login (`attendance.manage`, reason required).
- **Correct** a clock-in or clock-out time, including entering a missed clock-out (reason required).
- **Remove** an entry that was made in error (reason required). It's kept and marked as removed, never deleted.

**Owners and admins** set the two flag limits on the same page (`tenant.settings.manage`): early or late tolerance (default 10 minutes, D5) and when an open entry counts as a missed clock-out (default 2 hours after the shift, D7).

## 2. Statuses and flags

Flags are **worked out when the page is shown**, not stored, so changing the tolerance updates them. They never change a recorded time (D4, D5).

| Flag | When |
|---|---|
| No shift | clocked in with no matching published shift |
| Late in / Early in | clock-in more than the tolerance after or before the shift start |
| Left early / Stayed late | clock-out more than the tolerance before or after the shift end |
| Missed clock-out | still clocked in longer than the limit after the shift end (12 hours after clocking in, with no shift) |
| Corrected | a manager recorded or changed the times |

Who's in shows one status per person: **Not in yet**, **Late** (shift started more than the tolerance ago, no clock-in), **In**, **On break**, **Done**, or **Missed clock-out**.

## 3. Data model (migration `20261108120000_attendance_clocking`)

All tables are tenant-owned, with `tenant_id`, RLS (enabled, forced, tenant policy), `UNIQUE(tenant_id, id)` and composite foreign keys. The app role has SELECT and INSERT, column-level UPDATE and no DELETE.

| Table | Holds | Rules in the database |
|---|---|---|
| `attendance_settings` | one row per organisation: tolerance minutes (0–60), missed clock-out hours (1–12) | one per organisation |
| `time_entry` | employee, site, shift (optional), clock in, clock out (empty while clocked in), source (`self`/`manager`), removed at, by | clock out after clock in, at most 24 hours; **at most one open entry per person**; the shift must be at the same site (composite key to a new `shift (tenant_id, id, site_id)` unique index) |
| `time_break` | entry, start, end (empty while on break) | end after start; at most one open break per entry |
| `time_entry_change` | append-only history: what happened (`recorded`, `corrected`, `removed`), old and new times, the reason, who and when | a trigger blocks UPDATE and DELETE for every role |

There are no pay columns. Times are `timestamptz`.

**Permissions:** `attendance.view` and `attendance.manage` are added to the catalogue and to every organisation's built-in owner, admin, HR manager and site manager roles (scope §4). `attendance.approve` and `attendance.export` come with M11.2 and M11.3.

## 4. Security

- **Ownership, not roles, for staff.** Clocking acts only on the employee record linked to the signed-in member. Staff can't name an employee, site, shift or time.
- **Server time only.** Clock-in, break and clock-out times come from the server.
- **Site scope.** Managers see and change entries only at sites where they hold the permission. A record from another site or organisation returns "not found".
- **Race safety.** The "one open entry" and "one open break" rules are database indexes, so a double tap can't create two.
- **Audit.** Every clock event, record, correction and removal writes an audit event with ids only. Reasons stay on `time_entry_change`, never in the audit trail (project rule).
- **No location, photos or device details** are collected (D2).
