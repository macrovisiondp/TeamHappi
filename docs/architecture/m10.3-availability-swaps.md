# M10.3: Availability, drops and swaps

This is the last part of the approved Rota scope (`m10-rota-scope.md`: "Availability, change requests and shift swaps where enabled"). The product owner asked to concentrate on TeamHappi and continue on 16 November 2026.

## Design choices (within the approved scope)

- **Unavailability is a warning, not a block.** It works like the other assignment warnings: a manager can still assign the person, with a written reason. Staff are told this plainly.
- **A "swap" hands a shift to a named colleague.** The colleague accepts first, then a manager approves, and every assignment check runs. A two-way trade (my Tuesday for your Thursday) isn't included; it can come later if wanted.

## What it does

- **Staff, on My shifts:**
  - **When you can't work:**
    - **Weekly:** a weekday, either all day or between two times (for example "Tuesdays, 18:00 to 23:00").
    - **Specific dates:** a range of up to a year, from today onwards, either all day or between times.
    - **Note:** optional, for the manager.
    - **Remove:** you can only remove your own entries.
  - **Ask to drop:** on your own published, upcoming shift. A manager decides, and you stay on it until then.
  - **Ask to swap:** you choose a colleague at that shift's site who has a linked login. They accept or decline under "Shifts offered to you", then a manager decides.
  - **Request status** shows on the shift, and you can withdraw it. There's one open request per shift.
- **Managers, on the Rota page:**
  - **Warnings and day view:**
    - assigning someone during a time they marked shows **"They said they can't work then (…)"**, which needs a reason;
    - each day lists who's unavailable ("Unavailable: Ana (all day)").
  - **Requests on each shift:**
    - "X asked to drop this shift."
    - "X wants to swap this to Y, who accepted."
    - "X offered this to Y (waiting for them to accept)."

    Managers can **Approve** or **Decline**. Approve only appears once a swap has been accepted.
  - **What approval does:**
    - **a drop:** takes the person off, so the shift is open and they see it under "No longer yours";
    - **a swap:** assigns the colleague through the normal assignment, so the right-to-work and double-booking blocks apply, and warnings need a reason.
  - **Other changes:** assigning someone else, making the shift open, or cancelling it **cancels any open request** on it.
  - **The week summary** counts open drop and swap requests.

## Privacy and audit

- **Staff access** is by ownership of their linked record; there's no new permission.
- **Managers** act with `rota.manage` at the site.
- **The swap list** shows names of linked colleagues at the person's own site, so they can pick one. Open shifts and their own shift list still never show who else is working.
- **Audit (ids and codes only, never notes or names):**
  - `rota.unavailability_added` / `_removed`;
  - `rota.drop_requested`, `rota.swap_requested`;
  - `rota.swap_accepted` / `rota.swap_refused`;
  - `rota.change_withdrawn`, `rota.change_approved`, `rota.change_declined`.

## Database: `20261107120000_rota_availability`

- **`unavailability`:**
  - weekly or a date range; all day or between times; an optional note;
  - removal is recorded, not deleted.
- **`shift_change_request`:**
  - drop or swap, with the person asking and, for a swap, the colleague;
  - status: pending, then accepted (swap only), then approved, declined, withdrawn or cancelled;
  - one open request per shift.
- **Both tables have:**
  - `tenant_id`;
  - RLS enabled and forced, with a tenant policy;
  - `UNIQUE(tenant_id, id)`, composite keys and CHECKs;
  - SELECT and INSERT, column-level UPDATE, and **no DELETE**.
- **What can't change:** who, which shift and which kind.
- **No changes to existing tables or permissions.**

## Tests

- **Unit** (`tests/unit/rota-policy.test.ts`, 4 new):
  - weekly all-day, including overnight shifts;
  - weekly between times;
  - date ranges including both ends;
  - plain-English descriptions.
- **Security** (`tests/security/availability-swaps.test.ts`, 7 tests, as the app role):
  - validation;
  - the warning and the day list;
  - only the owner removes an entry;
  - drop: your own shift only, once; other organisations and staff can't decide; approval opens the shift;
  - swap targets: linked colleagues at the site only; the colleague must accept before approval; only they can answer;
  - approval runs the right-to-work block, and decline works;
  - withdraw: your own only;
  - a manager's change cancels open requests;
  - the audit holds no names or notes;
  - no DELETE, fixed columns, and the kind CHECKs.
- **Browser** (`tests/e2e/my-shifts.spec.ts`, extended): the new starter adds dates they can't work, asks to drop a shift, the manager approves, and it shows under "No longer yours". It passes on desktop and mobile.
- **Results:**
  - full check: 571 tests and the build pass;
  - Rota, My shifts and app-screens browser tests pass. The Rota retries were the sign-in rate limit; one accessibility scan was slow once.
