# M7.3: Employee onboarding

The third increment of the approved M7 scope (`m7-onboarding-scope.md`): link a person's record to a TeamHappi login through an invitation, give them a phone-first "My onboarding" page, and let them acknowledge policies themselves. It also closes the M6 follow-up "Link an invitation to an existing person record".

## What it does

- **Invite from a person's record** (owners and admins, who hold `member.invite` organisation-wide):
  - **Where:** the person page has a "TeamHappi login" card. **Invite to TeamHappi** opens the usual invite form, with their email and primary site filled in and the Worker role by default.
  - **What you get:** a one-time link, copied and sent as in M6. Pending invitations show "For <name>" in Settings → Members.
  - **When it's refused:** another organisation's record, people who have left, records already linked, and a second open invitation for the same person.
- **Linking on acceptance:** the record is linked to the new login (`employee.membership_id`) inside `app.accept_invitation`.
  - **When it's skipped:** the record was linked meanwhile, the person has left, or the login is already linked to another record. They still join the organisation; only the link is skipped.
  - **Audit:** the link is audited as `employee.login_linked`.
  - **No other way in:** the app role still can't change `membership_id`, and can't call the helper function.
- **My onboarding** (`/onboarding/me`, phone first):
  - **Who can see it:** it's reached by **ownership** (the record linked to the signed-in member), not by a role. Opening **Onboarding** without onboarding access, but with your own onboarding, takes you there. Everyone else still sees "Your role doesn't include onboarding".
  - **What it shows:**
    - progress across all steps (including the manager's, as numbers only);
    - "For you to do": their own tasks with guidance and due dates;
    - "Documents to bring": titles only;
    - "Done", marked "Recorded by your manager" where a manager did it on their behalf.
  - **What it never shows:** right-to-work details, "cleared to start" (it depends on right to work), exception reasons, notes, who checked documents, or anyone else's records.
- **Completing their own tasks:**
  - **Which tasks:** only their own, meaning employee-owned tasks on their own open onboarding. Anything else returns "not found".
  - **How it's recorded:** as done by them, not on their behalf, and audited with `byEmployee: true`.
- **Policy acknowledgements:** policy tasks need the box "I've read and understood …" ticked.
  - **The text acknowledged** is the task's guidance, which was copied when onboarding started, so later template edits don't change it.
  - **Audit:** `onboarding.policy_acknowledged`.
- **Managers completing an employee's task** still record it "on their behalf". The exception is when the manager *is* that employee, in which case it's their own completion.

## Database: `20261030120000_employee_onboarding`

- **`invitation.employee_id`** (nullable):
  - a composite foreign key to `employee`;
  - a partial unique index, so there's one open invitation per record.
- **App-role grants are unchanged.** The app inserts the column through its existing table-level INSERT. It still can't UPDATE `employee_id` or `employee.membership_id`.
- **`app.accept_invitation(hash)`** is replaced with `CREATE OR REPLACE`. The checks, outcomes and grants are the same as M6, plus a call to the linking step.
- **`app.link_invited_employee(...)`** is new. It's SECURITY DEFINER with a pinned `search_path`, and no login role can EXECUTE it, so it's only callable from `accept_invitation`.
- **Existing data is unchanged.**

## Tests

- **`tests/security/employee-onboarding.test.ts`** (real app role):
  - **Inviting:** a record from another organisation, a malformed id, someone who has left, and a second open invitation for the same person are all refused; the creation audit carries the record's id.
  - **Linking:** acceptance links the record and audits it; a login already linked elsewhere joins without a link; the app can't change links or call the link function.
  - **My onboarding:** shows only their own tasks and documents, with no restricted data, and still no onboarding access by role; they complete their own tasks, and policies need an acknowledgement; a manager's completion stays "on their behalf"; manager tasks, colleagues' tasks and other organisations' tasks are refused; people without a linked record see nothing; cancelled onboarding is hidden.
- **`tests/e2e/employee-onboarding.spec.ts`** (desktop and mobile, axe on every screen):
  1. The owner starts onboarding, then invites the person from their record (email filled in), and sees "For <name>" in Members.
  2. The new starter creates an account from the link, opens **Onboarding**, lands on **My onboarding**, ticks and confirms the handbook policy, and sees it under Done.
  3. The owner sees the record linked and the task done, not "on their behalf".
- **Regression:** the M6 invitation browser tests and security tests pass against the replaced function.

## Not in M7.3

- **Linking someone who is already a member.** Invitations refuse existing members, as in M6. Linking an existing login needs its own small admin action later.
- **Email delivery of invitations and reminders** (G6).
- **Push notifications and an installable app** (the Release 1 PWA work).
- **Employees uploading documents** (G7).
