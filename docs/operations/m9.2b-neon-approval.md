## Effect on Joemis Kitchen and other organisations

- **What changes:** application pages get a CV section.
  - Until S3 is switched on, it says "CV uploads aren't switched on yet".
  - After that, people who manage recruitment at the site can upload a PDF or .docx (up to 4 MB, 5 per application).
- **What doesn't change:** nothing else. No existing records are touched.

## Rollback

- **Atomic:** the migration runs as one transaction.
- **While no CV has been uploaded,** reversing it means:
  - `DROP TABLE application_document;`
  - `DROP FUNCTION app.application_document_guard();`

  That would be a separately approved run.
- **Code rollback without a database rollback is safe:** older code ignores the table.
- **Files already in S3** would need deleting from the bucket by hand, under `tenants/`.
