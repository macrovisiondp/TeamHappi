## Effect on Joemis Kitchen and other organisations

- **For staff with a linked login:** they can say when they can't work, ask to drop a shift, or offer it to a colleague.
- **For managers:** they see who's unavailable each day, get a warning when assigning in those times, and approve or decline drops and swaps on the Rota page.

## Rollback

- **Atomic:** the migration runs as one transaction.
- **While both tables are empty,** reversing it means `DROP TABLE shift_change_request, unavailability;`. That would be a separately approved run.
- **Code rollback without a database rollback is safe:** older code ignores both tables.
