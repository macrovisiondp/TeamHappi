## Effect on Joemis Kitchen and other organisations

- **For staff with a linked login:** a "Clock in" card at the top of My shifts, with breaks and clock out, and their own times for the last 14 days.
- **For managers:** the Attendance page goes live: who's in at each site and day, flags (no shift, early or late, missed clock-out, corrected), and recording, correcting or removing times with a reason.
- **For owners and admins:** two flag settings (default 10 minutes and 2 hours).
- **Nothing collects location, photos or device details.** No pay is calculated.

## Rollback

- **Atomic:** the migration runs as one transaction.
- **While the new tables are empty,** reversing it is the SQL listed at the top of the migration file. That would be a separately approved run.
- **Code rollback without a database rollback is safe:** older code ignores the new tables and permissions.
