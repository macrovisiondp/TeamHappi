## Before it goes live: gate G4

Approving this migration (G3) **doesn't** approve the right-to-work wording and rules. Before real right-to-work checks are recorded, a qualified adviser should review them, or you should explicitly accept the risk of going without that review (G4). The items to review:

- **Wording:** the method, list and outcome labels, and the explanation shown on the record.
- **Consistency rules:** List A means confirmed, List B means time-limited, identity service checks are continuous only, and a confirmed check needs the person present.
- **"Covers the date":** a check covers a date until permission ends, even when the follow-up is overdue.
- **Follow-up window:** 60 days.
- **Retention:** the proposed period is employment plus 2 years; nothing is deleted automatically.

The full list is in `docs/architecture/m7.2-onboarding-compliance.md`.

## Rollback considerations

- The migration runs as one transaction: if any statement fails, nothing is applied.
- **While both tables are empty**, it can be reversed with the statements in the migration's header comment. That would be a separately approved, reviewed run.
- **Once right-to-work checks exist, never roll back.** That would delete compliance records. Fix forward with a new migration.
- **Code rollback without a database rollback is safe:** older app versions don't read the new tables.
